### Added

- First release of `wiswa-mcp`, a FastMCP server exposing Wiswa settings discovery to AI
  assistants.
- MCP tools `get_defaults`, `lookup_setting`, `list_settings`, and `search_settings` for querying
  Wiswa defaults.
